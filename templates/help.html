How do I create an account?

Choose Register in the top navigation, enter your name and email, and create a password with at least 8 characters. This demo stores a password hash in SQLite.

Can I book an appointment here?

Not yet. This starter project has informational pages, account registration/login, and a contact form. It does not make or confirm appointments.

Where does my contact message go?

It is stored in the local hospital.db SQLite file on the computer running the app. No email is sent.

Is this website for emergencies?

No. This is a learning project and is not monitored by hospital staff. For an emergency, contact your local emergency service or go to the nearest emergency department.

Is my information ready for production use?

No. Do not enter real patient or health information. A production deployment needs a security review, CSRF protection, access controls, secure configuration, backups, audit logs, and privacy/compliance review.
